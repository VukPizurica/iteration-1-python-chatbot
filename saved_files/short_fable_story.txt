Once upon a time, in a forest filled with tall trees, a wise old owl lived. The owl loved to tell stories to the other animals. One day, a young rabbit asked the owl to tell him a story about courage. The owl said, "Bravery is not the absence of fear, but the judgment that something else is more important than fear." The rabbit thought about this for a moment, then asked, "What is more important than fear?" The owl replied, "Wisdom, kindness, and the well-being of others are all more important than fear." The rabbit felt inspired by the owl's words and decided to face his fears. From that day on, the rabbit stood up for himself and for others, and he lived a happy and fulfilling life. The owl was proud of the rabbit and continued to share his wisdom with all the animals in the forest.
